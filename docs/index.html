Clips Uploader

A personal tool that uploads short videos to social media accounts owned by its developer. It is not offered to other users.

Privacy policy

The tool only accesses accounts owned by its developer, after the developer signs in. It stores the access tokens those sign-ins return, encrypted, in the developer's own cloud storage, and uses them only to upload the developer's videos and read their basic account info and publishing status. No data about other people is collected, sold or shared.

To remove access, revoke the app in the account's settings (Instagram: Apps and websites; TikTok: Security and permissions; Google: Third-party access). Questions: contact the repository owner on GitHub.

Terms of use

This tool is for the developer's own use. It is provided as is, without warranty. Each upload follows the terms of the platform it is sent to.
